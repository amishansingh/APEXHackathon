# Prompt: North American Trade Sector Map (Central Graphic)

## What it is
The hero graphic that sits directly below the header, spanning the full page width. It is a stylised celestial / astronomical map of the North American trade sector showing three markets — Canada, United States, and Mexico — as orbital bodies connected by constellation lines.

## Visual description
- **Background:** deep navy (#0d1a35), no border
- **Two dashed ellipses** centred on the United States node, drawn in brass (#d9a659) at low opacity (55% and 32%), suggesting orbital rings around the largest market
- **Three market nodes** — solid brass circles, sized by market importance:
  - **United States** (centre): largest circle (~20 px radius), with two concentric orbital rings around it
  - **Canada** (upper-left, slightly offset): medium circle (~12 px radius) with one faint ring
  - **Mexico** (lower-right): smallest circle (~10 px radius) with one faint ring
- **Dashed connecting lines** between Canada→US and US→Mexico, in brass at 70% opacity, suggesting constellation or trade route lines
- **Market labels** below each node: italic Georgia/serif font, cream (#f2ead0), 11–12 px, letter-spacing 1.5px
- **Ambient stars:** small cream and brass dots scattered in the empty space — varying sizes (0.6–1.5 px radius) at varying opacities, giving the map a star-chart quality
- **Caption below:** "North American Trade Sector · US · CA · MX" in brass, small italic serif, all-caps or mixed, wide letter-spacing

## Mood
Antique celestial atlas. Looks like a 17th-century astronomical chart or a constellation map, but shows supply chain markets instead of stars. Quiet, authoritative, slightly mysterious.

## Dimensions
Wide landscape format: approximately 620×200 px (3:1 ratio). Can be wider for slides.

## Copilot prompt
> An antique celestial star chart / astronomical map on a deep navy background (#0d1a35). Three glowing brass-gold circles of different sizes represent trade markets — largest in the centre labelled "United States" in small italic serif text below it, medium circle upper-left labelled "Canada", small circle lower-right labelled "Mexico". Two large dashed ellipses in warm gold orbit the central circle. Dashed gold connecting lines link the three circles like constellation lines. Small cream and gold star dots scattered across the background. Minimal, engraved, antique navigation aesthetic. Wide landscape format, no border.
