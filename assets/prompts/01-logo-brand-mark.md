# Prompt: Cosmic Mart Brand Mark (Logo)

## What it is
The primary logo / brand mark for "Cosmic Mart — Celestial Inventory Atlas". It appears in the top-left of the dashboard header alongside the wordmark.

## Visual description
A compass rose / astrolabe rendered as a minimal line drawing. Two concentric circles form the outer ring. Inside, four diamond-shaped cardinal points in filled brass — a tall narrow diamond pointing up/down (north–south needle) and a wide flat diamond pointing left/right (east–west needle). They cross at the centre. Faint diagonal lines extend at 45° from the centre outward, at lower opacity, suggesting the eight-point compass. The overall shape is a classic nautical or celestial navigation instrument.

## Style
- Line art only — no fills except the compass needle diamonds
- Stroke weight: very fine (1 px at 40×40 px)
- Palette: warm brass / gold (#d9a659) on deep navy (#0d1a35) background
- No drop shadow, no glow, no texture
- Feels engraved — precise, antique, measured

## Dimensions
Square. 40×40 px in the dashboard; 200×200 px or larger for presentations and print.

## Copilot prompt
> A minimal line-art compass rose logo on a deep navy blue background (#0d1a35). Two concentric thin circles. Inside, a four-pointed compass star formed by two overlapping narrow diamond shapes — one pointing north–south, one pointing east–west — filled with warm brass gold (#d9a659). Faint diagonal lines at 45 degrees from the centre at low opacity. No text. No shadow. Engraved antique celestial navigation aesthetic. Square format, centred, clean white space around the mark.
