# Prompt: Agent Sigil Icons (Set of 7)

## What they are
Small line-art icons used to identify each agent type throughout the dashboard. Appear at 20×20 px inline with agent names. All share the same visual language: thin stroke, no fill (except a few small filled elements), cream (#f2ead0) on transparent background.

---

## Icon 1 — Cultural Signal (crescent moon)

**Description:** A crescent moon shape. A full circle with a smaller circle overlapping from the upper-right, creating the crescent cutout. The visible crescent is fully filled.

**Copilot prompt:**
> A minimal flat icon of a crescent moon. Solid filled crescent shape, no outline, no texture. Cream white (#f2ead0) on transparent background. 24×24 px viewbox. Clean, geometric, single colour.

---

## Icon 2 — Climate Signal (sun with rays)

**Description:** A small filled circle in the centre (the sun disc). Eight short straight lines radiating outward at equal intervals — four cardinal and four diagonal. Line-drawn, no fill on the rays.

**Copilot prompt:**
> A minimal line-art sun icon. Small solid circle in the centre with eight short radiating lines (four cardinal, four diagonal). Thin stroke, no fill on rays. Cream white on transparent background. 24×24 px. Clean geometric style.

---

## Icon 3 — Social Signal (broadcast arcs)

**Description:** A small filled dot in the lower-left corner. Three arcs of increasing radius emanate from it to the upper-right, like a wifi or broadcast signal symbol. Line-drawn, no fill.

**Copilot prompt:**
> A minimal line-art broadcast / signal icon. A small solid dot in the lower-left with three concentric arcs radiating outward to the upper-right, like a wifi symbol rotated. Thin stroke. Cream white on transparent background. 24×24 px.

---

## Icon 4 — Economic Signal (bar chart)

**Description:** Three solid vertical rectangles of increasing height, left to right, like a simple bar chart. Fully filled, no outline.

**Copilot prompt:**
> A minimal flat bar chart icon. Three solid vertical bars of ascending height from left to right. No outline, fully filled. Cream white on transparent background. 24×24 px.

---

## Icon 5 — Local News (document / page)

**Description:** A rounded rectangle representing a page. Three horizontal lines inside it at different heights and lengths represent text lines. Line-drawn — the page outline is a stroke, the text lines are strokes.

**Copilot prompt:**
> A minimal line-art document icon. A rectangle with slightly rounded corners. Three horizontal lines inside at different lengths representing text. Thin stroke throughout. Cream white on transparent background. 24×24 px.

---

## Icon 6 — Stock / Orbital (planet with ring)

**Description:** A small filled circle in the centre (a planet or node). A dashed ellipse orbits it at an angle, rotated about 20° from horizontal, like a planetary orbital path.

**Copilot prompt:**
> A minimal line-art orbital / planet icon. A small solid circle in the centre with a dashed ellipse ring tilted at roughly 20 degrees around it, like a planet and its orbit. Thin dashed stroke. Cream white on transparent background. 24×24 px.

---

## Icon 7 — Velocity / Arrow Trail (motion arrow)

**Description:** Two small filled circles on the left side at different sizes (suggesting motion trail), followed by a horizontal arrow line pointing right, with an arrowhead formed by two diagonal lines.

**Copilot prompt:**
> A minimal line-art motion arrow icon. Two small solid dots on the left of different sizes suggesting a trail, followed by a rightward-pointing arrow line with a simple chevron arrowhead. Thin stroke. Cream white on transparent background. 24×24 px.

---

## Shared style rules for all icons
- Viewbox: 24×24
- Stroke width: 1.2–1.6 px
- Stroke linecap: round
- Colour: cream (#f2ead0) — or output as white on transparent for flexibility
- No drop shadow, no gradient, no texture
- Flat / minimal — think Feather Icons or Lucide but slightly more geometric

## Batch prompt (for generating all 7 at once)
> A set of 7 minimal line-art icons in matching style, cream white on transparent background, 24×24 px each. All share thin round-cap strokes and a flat geometric style with no gradients or shadows. The icons are: (1) filled crescent moon, (2) sun with eight radiating lines and a centre dot, (3) broadcast arcs from a lower-left dot, (4) three ascending solid bars (bar chart), (5) document rectangle with three text lines, (6) planet with a dashed tilted orbital ring, (7) two trailing dots followed by a rightward arrow. Arrange them in a row.
