# Prompt: Presentation Slide Background

## What it is
A full-bleed background for presentation slides that matches the dashboard's visual language — deep navy with a subtle brass nebula glow and faint star field.

## Visual description
- **Base:** deep navy blue (#0d1a35) filling the entire frame
- **Brass nebula glow:** a very soft, large radial gradient ellipse in warm brass/amber centred near the top-left corner (~20–25% from left, ~15–20% from top). Colour at centre: rgba(217,166,89,0.10) — barely visible, just enough to feel warm. Fades completely to transparent by the midpoint of the slide.
- **Cool blue-grey accent:** a second very faint radial gradient near the bottom-right corner (~75–80% from left, ~80% from top). Colour: rgba(160,190,220,0.05) — cooler and even subtler than the brass. Suggests depth.
- **Edge vignette:** the corners and edges fade slightly darker than the centre — rgba(6,12,26,0.45) radially from the edges inward.
- **Star field (optional):** a handful of tiny dots at varying opacities scattered across the frame. Cream (#f2ead0) at 50–90% opacity, sizes 1–2 px. Roughly 8–12 dots total — sparse, not a galaxy.

## What it should NOT have
- No text, no logo (those are added as separate slide elements)
- No hard edges or obvious gradients
- No blue glow, purple, or saturated colours
- No noise or grain texture

## Aspect ratios needed
- 16:9 (standard widescreen presentation)
- 4:3 (if needed for older projectors)

## Copilot prompt
> A deep navy blue presentation slide background (#0d1a35). A very subtle warm brass-gold radial glow (barely 10% opacity) in the upper-left corner, fading completely to transparent. A second extremely faint cool blue-grey glow in the lower-right corner. Dark vignette at the edges making the centre slightly lighter. A sparse scatter of 8–10 tiny cream white dots of varying sizes (star field). No text. No hard gradients. Cinematic, quiet, like a night sky over the ocean. 16:9 format.
